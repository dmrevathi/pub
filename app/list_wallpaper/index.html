List Wallpaper – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Summary

List Wallpaper lets you write a short list (for example, a shopping list) and, if you choose, draws it onto your lock screen or home screen wallpaper. The app does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, phone number, email, contacts or location. Your list never leaves your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information as described below.

Information the app stores on your phone

  • Your list: the items you type are saved only on your phone so they are still there when you open the app again.
  • Your wallpaper choice: if you turn on the wallpaper feature, the app keeps a copy of the picture you pick (or of your current wallpaper) inside the app, so it can put your wallpaper back when the list is cleared.
  • Your settings, such as whether the list is shown on the lock screen or home screen.

All of this stays on your phone. It is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

  • Set wallpaper: used only to draw your list onto your wallpaper and to put your original wallpaper back.
  • Read storage (Android 12 and older only): asked only if you choose "Use current phone wallpaper", so the app can read your current wallpaper once and put it back later.
  • Pictures you pick: when you choose "Choose from gallery", the app reads only the one picture you select.

Information sent over the internet

  • Your list, your pictures and your wallpaper are not sent anywhere.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): the app shows a banner ad inside the app (never on your lock screen or wallpaper). To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalised. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google's consent form.

Your choices

  • You can turn the wallpaper feature on or off at any time in the app's Settings.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on your phone).
  • You can remove everything the app has stored by clearing its data or uninstalling it.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Apart from what Google AdMob collects as described above, we do not share information with anyone.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
